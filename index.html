The Approach

How I think about
automation.

Build for the full pipeline, not just the trigger

Every automation I build covers the complete flow — from the first webhook to the final output. I design for edge cases, branching logic, and error handling so workflows don't break silently in production.

AI belongs inside the workflow, not on top of it

I embed AI models — OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter — directly into the logic layer. Scoring leads, generating content, answering queries from a live knowledge base. AI does the thinking; the pipeline does the moving.

Real pain points drive real automation

8+ years in customer operations taught me where the manual work actually hides — ticket routing, follow-up emails, CRM updates, lead reviews. I automate the tasks people hate most, not just the ones that look impressive.

Tools are interchangeable. Logic is not.

n8n, Make.com, Zapier — the platform is a delivery vehicle. The conditional routing, data transformation, and JavaScript code nodes are where the real system lives. I build logic that works regardless of what's underneath.

Ship fast, then make it bulletproof

I get working automations into production quickly, then layer in structured error handling, retry logic, and monitoring. A live system that catches issues beats a perfect system still on the whiteboard.

Every workflow is a system, not a script

I connect CRMs, Google Workspace, Slack, and AI APIs into coherent systems with clear data flow. Not isolated automations — interconnected pipelines where each piece informs the next.
